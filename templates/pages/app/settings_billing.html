How billing works

One managed job is one book order. The price is fixed by the estimated page count and never by tokens. Failed or cancelled jobs are not charged. If you connect your own API, nothing is charged by Book Fork. Payment is completed on Gumroad and verified by Book Fork before your book is queued; the browser return URL is never trusted.
